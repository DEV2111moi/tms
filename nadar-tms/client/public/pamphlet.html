📋 Comprehensive System Overview

The Nadar TMS replaces legacy manual logs with an integrated cloud command center. Developed specifically for TMHNU educational institutions (including Nadar Saraswathi College of Arts & Science, Engineering, Education, and Matriculation Schools), this platform unifies institutional administration, daily workshop servicing, driver governance, student tracking, and financial compliance into a single cohesive interface.

⚙️ 10 Core Enterprise Modules
🚌
1. Fleet & Vehicle Master

Complete vehicle profiles for 120+ buses and vans with registration numbers, seating capacities, RC Book vault, chassis/engine records, and dedicated Spare Bus stand-by pool.

Digital RC Vault Spare Bus Pool Odometer Tracking
📋
2. Workshop & TMHNU Pink Job Cards

Faithful digital replication of the official TMHNU Pink Service Slip. Captures Lamp, Mileage, S.No, Mechanic & Driver signatures, service details, and dual-mode billing print.

Pink Slip Print Bill Invoice Bus Service History
📦
3. Spare Parts Store & Inventory

Warehouse storekeeper module tracking parts stock, SKU codes, unit purchase costs, and minimum reorder alerts. Automatically deducts inventory upon service billing.

Auto Stock Deduction Restocking Logs Min Stock Alert
⛽
4. Diesel & Fuel Efficiency Analytics

Audits fuel pump fillings, liters pumped, and total bill amounts against odometer readings. Automatically calculates exact km/L mileage to detect fuel pilferage.

km/L Mileage Fuel Theft Detection Cost / KM
👨‍✈️
5. Driver Dossier & Payroll HR

Comprehensive driver database tracking Aadhaar, EPF/ESI, HCV license expiry, and badge renewals. Computes monthly salary, trip allowances, and printable payslips.

License Expiry Radar EPF/ESI Salary Slips
🗺️
6. Route Logistics & Multi-Shift Stops

Geographic stop sequencing with pickup timestamps and GPS points. Supports Morning Shift 1, Morning Shift 2, Evening Return, and weekend special trip schedules.

Multi-Shift Routing Stop Timings Initial Point Log
🔄
7. Shift Allocation & Breakdown Swaps

Real-time triple assignment: Route + Bus + Driver + Incharge. Includes 1-click dynamic substitution to swap breakdown vehicles with reserve spare buses on-the-fly.

Dynamic Substitution Breakdown Dispatch Live Alert
📱
8. Incharge Attendance & Parent Tracker

Dedicated mobile attendance portal for bus in-charges to mark student boarding stop-by-stop. Parents enjoy real-time bus status and estimated arrival times (ETA).

1-Tap Boarding Parent ETA App RFID/QR Ready
🛞
9. Axle-Wise Tyre Lifecycle Tracking

Position-by-position tyre management (Front, Rear-Dual, Spare). Audits fitted odometer km, current run distance, and original vs retreaded quality to prevent blowouts.

Axle Position Map Retreading Log Run-KM Counter
🔔
10. FC & Compliance Expiry Radar

Automated legal radar tracking Fitness Certificate (FC), Insurance, Road Permits, and PUC dates. Generates priority alerts 30, 15, and 7 days prior to expiry.

100% Legal Compliance Zero Impound Risk Automated Alert
